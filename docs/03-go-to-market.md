# Go-to-Market

## 1. Positioning

**Category**: Intent engineering (we are naming it, and we will keep saying it).
**One-liner**: Bison makes autonomous coding agents build the right system, safely,
the first time.
**Against big consultancies**: we ship specs, code, tests, and evidence, not decks.
**Against LLM app agencies**: we sit upstream of implementation; they are the commodity
we help clients stop paying for.
**Against eval/safety vendors**: we integrate verification into architecture and CI;
a dashboard without an enforcement point is a wish.

## 2. Ideal customer profile

Primary: **VP Engineering / CTO at a 50–500 engineer company** that has adopted
coding agents in the last 12 months and has one of these visible pains:

- Incident or rework rate rose after agent adoption.
- A multi-agent product is drifting, looping, or over budget.
- Compliance, security, or a customer has asked for evidence of AI safety controls.
- A regulated launch (payments, health, legal, insurance, logistics) is blocked.

Trigger events to watch: new CTO, agentic product announcement, AI-related incident,
regulatory deadline, Series B/C, hiring for "AI platform" roles.

Disqualifiers: no executive sponsor, no accountable owner will be named, wants
hourly staff augmentation.

## 3. Messaging pillars

1. **The bottleneck moved.** Code is cheap; intent, topology, and validation are not.
2. **Faster production of the wrong thing is not progress.**
3. **Evidence, not assurance.** Every safety claim has a test, a probe, and a signature.
4. **Humans stay accountable.** AI optimises execution; a named person owns the risk.

## 5. Sales process

1. **Intent review (free, 45 min)**: client brings one thing built wrong or one
   feature they cannot sign off. We diagnose live using the readiness scorecard and a
   whiteboard BIS. We end with a written one-page diagnosis within 24 hours.
2. **Proposal (fixed price, 2 pages)**: scope, do-not-build, deliverables, price,
   accountable role required, start date. No custom proposals over 2 pages.
3. **Close**: MSA + SOW signed electronically. 50% on start, 50% on evidence table.
4. **Deliver** per playbook.
5. **Expand**: day 14 handover includes the next-step proposal. Target: 50% of
   Sprints expand within 90 days.

Pipeline metrics tracked weekly: reviews booked, proposals sent, close rate, average
deal, expansion rate, referral rate.

## 6. Pricing tactics

- Intent Sprint is the fixed-price wedge; never discount below design-partner rate.
- Design-partner rate (35% off) for the first five clients per vertical, in
  exchange for a case study and reference calls.
- Bundles for multi-discipline needs (see site pricing page).
- Retainers priced so three retainers cover one pod; pushed at every handover.

## 7. Content engine

A weekly cadence, produced with the same agents we use for delivery:

- **Monday**: one pattern essay (1,000 words) derived from last week's engagement.
- **Wednesday**: one spec-defect story ("the agent asked X; here is what the spec
  was missing").
- **Friday**: BIS or Fence changelog.
- **Monthly**: readiness benchmark from anonymised scorecard results.
