How an engagement runs

Frame

Two days with your decision owner. Outcome statement, objective function, hard floors, do-not-build list.

Specify

Constraints with enforcement points. Edge cases with expected behaviours. Data contracts with provenance. Every line lint-checked against the BIS standard.

Prove

Our agents implement from the spec with no clarifying questions. If they have to ask, the spec failed and we fix the spec, not the code.

Verify

Property tests, security probes, and telemetry wired into CI. Evidence table filled. A human signs it.
