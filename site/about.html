About

We eat our own thesis.

Bison is a small firm of intent engineers, systems architects, and verification engineers. Autonomous agents do our implementation. Humans do the framing, the topology, and the signing.

Why "Bison"

Bison do not outrun storms. They turn and walk straight through them, which is the shortest path out. When the industry panics about AI replacing engineers, we walk into the part of engineering that got harder, not easier.

How we work

  • Pods of three. One intent engineer, one architect, one verification engineer per engagement.
  • Fixed scope. Change requests are new sprints, not surprises.
  • You keep everything. Specs, code, tests, dashboards, runbooks.
  • Every engagement improves the method. Anonymised patterns feed the BIS standard and Fence policy library.

Accountability statement

AI can optimise execution speed. Humans remain solely responsible for system safety, regulatory liability, and structural trust. Every Bison deliverable names the accountable human role for every class of automated decision, and every safety claim is backed by evidence, not assurance.
