Privacy

This website

The Spec Builder and Readiness Scorecard run entirely in your browser. Drafts and scores are stored in your browser's local storage and are not transmitted unless you choose to submit a form or provide an email address. If you provide an email, we store it with the context you supplied (tool used, spec title or score) to send the material you requested and, if you opted in, the fortnightly digest. You may unsubscribe at any time. We use privacy-respecting analytics without cross-site tracking.

Engagements

Client data stays in client environments. Bison agents run inside client infrastructure or in isolated per-client environments with no cross-client retention. Anonymised patterns (spec defects, failure modes) are retained under the terms of the engagement contract. A data-processing addendum is available for regulated clients.
