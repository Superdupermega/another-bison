Certification

Certified Intent Engineer

Within two years every engineering org running coding agents will hire for this role. We are defining it now: what it does, how it is examined, and who is on the registry.

The role

An intent engineer translates fuzzy business needs into specifications that autonomous agents implement without questions and verification systems can prove. They own the objective function, the constraints, the edge cases, the do-not-build list, and the accountability sign-off. They do not write production code by hand; if they have to, the spec was wrong.

The exam

You receive a deliberately messy brief. You produce a BIS spec in four hours. Your score is its Spec Sufficiency Score across three agents, plus a review of the constraints and accountability sections by two certified reviewers. Pass mark: SSS ≥ 75 and no failing lint rule.
