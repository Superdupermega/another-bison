Charter

Vendor-neutrality charter

Model providers will bundle spec elicitation and guardrails into their own products. They cannot be the party that verifies themselves. We can. These commitments make that credible.

We route across providers by default.

Every topology we design includes multi-vendor routing with equivalence tests. No client is locked to one lab by our work.

We take no referral fees, credits, or rebates from model vendors.

If a vendor relationship exists (marketplace listing, partnership), it is disclosed on this page and never influences routing or reports.

Our migration drift reports are unsponsored.

Public equivalence reports are funded by Bison, use published methodology, and name every model tested.

Attestations are evidence-based and vendor-blind.

Bison Attested states which properties were verified by which tests on which date. The model behind the system is a parameter, not a judgement.

We publish the method.

BIS, the SSS formula, the reference harness, and the Fence policy schema are open. Neutrality that cannot be inspected is a claim, not a charter.

Disclosed relationships: none at the time of publication.
